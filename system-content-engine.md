# System: Content Engine

*Last updated: May 2026 | Owner: Evan | Review cadence: Weekly creation, Monthly theme review*

---

## 1. Purpose

Run a consistent content machine that tells the hand-smoked, Ontario-made Phlippens story and keeps the Phlippen the Script mental health and conversation mission visible — without requiring the team to start from scratch every week. The content engine is not about volume. It is about showing up consistently with the right message for the right audience on the right three channels, and making every piece of content do double or triple duty across the ecosystem.

---

## 2. Scope

**In scope:**
- Instagram content for both Phlippens and Men Who Walk & Talk accounts
- Facebook Events for MWWT walks and key activations
- Klaviyo email (monthly broadcast + welcome sequence)
- Recipe content (short-form for Reels, longer form for email)
- Keep It Phlippen Real episode production support (scripting, structure, titles)
- Story cards and in-store POS copy

**Out of scope:**
- Paid media buying or boosted posts (not until organic story is proven)
- Deep PR (media pitching is a separate relationship-driven activity, not a content engine task)
- TikTok, LinkedIn, Twitter/X (deprioritized for bandwidth reasons — see `01-strategy-2026-roadmap.md`)
- Video production beyond phone-quality (phone is the standard; polished production is not the goal at this stage)

## 5. Workflow

### Content Pillars

Every piece of content maps to one of five pillars. When the week's focus is unclear, pick the pillar that's been underrepresented recently.

| Pillar | What it covers | Example formats |
|---|---|---|
| **Product** | The sauce itself — hand-smoked process, Ontario ingredients, versatility | Process Reel in the kitchen, "5 ways to use it" carousel, product close-up with context |
| **Purpose** | Phlippen the Script — the mental health mission, the villain (performance culture), the "take the lid off" message | Kris or Evan to camera, quote cards, Phlippen the Script partnership content |
| **Recipe** | Practical, honest, low-barrier recipes that show the product earning its place on the table | Recipe Reel (phone, real kitchen, no styling), step-by-step carousel, "what I made last night" posts |
| **Behind the scenes** | The real process — smoking, bottling, market runs, account visits, walks | Stories-first content, raw clips, honest voiceovers, founder day-in-the-life |
| **Community** | MWWT walks, the golf tournament, retail partner stories, customer reactions | Walk recap photos, retailer spotlight, tasting event moments, KIPR episode clips |

**Ratio target (per month):** 2 product, 1 purpose, 3 recipe, 2 behind-the-scenes, 2 community = ~10 posts/month across both accounts. Reels should make up at least 4 of those.

---

**Step 1 — Choose the week's focus (Monday, 15 min)**
Check the campaign calendar (`phlippens-ecosystem-campaign-plan.md`) for the week's assigned focus. If there's no strong campaign beat this week, review recent performance — which pillar has the most engagement? Which hasn't been touched in 2+ weeks?

**Step 2 — Draft content (Monday/Tuesday, 60–90 min)**
- Write the caption first. The caption is the message; the visual supports it.
- Draft the hook (first line of caption or first frame of Reel) — this is the most important sentence. It determines whether someone stops scrolling.
- For Reels: write a simple 3–5 step structure before filming. Kris shoots; Evan structures.
- For email: pull the week's best story thread and write 150–200 words around it. Keep one CTA only.
- Caption voice: Phlippens is warm, direct, slightly cheeky. MWWT is honest, low-pressure, bloke-ish. Never clinical. Never corporate.

**Step 3 — Founder review (Tuesday, 15 min)**
Kris reviews copy drafts — not for grammar, for truth. If it doesn't sound like something he'd actually say, it goes back. This is non-negotiable. The brand voice lives in Kris, not in the copy doc.

**Step 4 — Schedule or post (Tuesday/Wednesday)**
- Phlippens IG: post Tuesday or Wednesday for best engagement windows
- MWWT IG: walk recaps post within 24 hours of the walk; event invites post 5–7 days before
- Facebook Events: created at least 7 days before the walk
- Reels: post natively (not through a scheduler if possible — native posting gets better reach)

**Step 5 — Weekly performance pulse (Friday, 15 min)**
Check Instagram Insights for any posts that went live this week. Note reach, saves, and comments. Anything that over-performed? Note the format and hook for reuse. Anything that under-performed? Note the pillar and format — avoid repeating.

**Step 6 — Monthly content retrospective (first Monday of each month, 30 min)**
- Top 3 posts by reach and by engagement rate — what do they have in common?
- Email open rate and click rate vs. prior month
- Which pillars generated the most responses? Which felt flat?
- Is the content calendar realistic for the coming month? Adjust if it's not.

## 6. Cadence

**Weekly:**
- Monday: choose focus, brief Kris on any filming needs
- Tuesday: draft copy, founder review
- Wednesday: post live, Facebook Event created if needed
- Friday: performance pulse check

**Monthly:**
- Content themes confirmed for the coming month (aligns with campaign calendar and seasonal moments)
- Monthly email drafted and sent in last week of the month
- Retrospective: what worked, what to drop, what to try

**Quarterly:**
- Content pillar review — are the five pillars still the right five? Any new story angles emerging?
- KIPR episode plan for the coming quarter — how many, what format, who are the subjects?
- Channel strategy check — are three channels still the right three, or has something emerged that warrants attention?

## 10. AI Opportunities

**Weekly content copilot:**
Give Claude the week's focus pillar, the hook, and one real story from the week (a tasting reaction, a line from a walk, a quote from the chef). Ask it to draft 3 caption options in Phlippens voice or MWWT voice. Kris picks one, edits to his voice, posts. Cuts drafting time from 45 minutes to 10.

**Reel script structure:**
Describe the recipe or story in plain language. Ask Claude to structure it as a 30–60 second Reel script: hook, 3–5 visual beats, CTA. Phone notes-quality input produces a workable structure immediately.

**Monthly performance summary:**
Paste the Instagram Insights export and Klaviyo report into Claude. Ask: "Summarize what worked this month, what didn't, and suggest one change to the content strategy for next month." Turns a spreadsheet into a decision.

**Email drafting:**
Describe the month's main story in one paragraph. Ask Claude to write the email in Phlippens voice: 150–200 words, one story, one CTA, subject line options included. First draft in 2 minutes.

**KIPR episode planning:**
Give Claude the subject (e.g., the restaurant chef, a retailer, a walk regular) and ask it to generate 8–10 interview questions that will draw out the real story — not the polished version. Documentary-style, not podcast-style.
