# Phlippens Operating Cadence

*Last updated: May 2026 | Owner: Kris (Founder) | Review cadence: Quarterly*

---

## 1. Purpose

A small team with big ambitions runs out of time before it runs out of ideas. The operating cadence is the answer to that problem. It is not a system of bureaucracy — it is a set of recurring moments where the important things get done consistently, before urgency makes them feel impossible. For Phlippens, a two-person team running production, sales, content, and community, the cadence is what turns strategy into habit. Without it, weeks pass, KPIs go unchecked, and the next tasting event gets planned the day before it happens. With it, the business has a pulse — regular, predictable, and strong enough to sustain growth.

The cadence also creates accountability without a hierarchy. When Kris and Evan agree on a weekly rhythm, neither person needs to manage the other — the calendar does it.

## 2. Cadence Overview

| Rhythm | Focus | Time investment |
|---|---|---|
| **Weekly** | Execution, content, sales activity, quick check-in | ~2–3 hours total across all weekly rituals |
| **Monthly** | KPI review, inventory/cash check, content retrospective | ~2 hours total |
| **Quarterly** | Strategy review, OKR refresh, big bets and corrections | ~3–4 hours total |
| **Ad hoc** | Decision reviews triggered by guardrails | As needed |

## 3. Weekly Rhythm

The weekly cadence assumes a standard work week. It is designed to be lightweight — most rituals take 15–30 minutes — and the total time cost is low. The goal is not to add meetings; it is to replace reactive chaos with intentional routine.

### 3.1 Monday — Plan and Prepare

**Production planning (Kris — 20 min):**
- Review pending orders from the weekend
- Confirm production run for the week based on inventory and reorder queue
- Flag any stockout risks to top accounts

**Content batching (Evan — 30 min):**
- Review this week's content calendar slot (from `phlippens-ecosystem-campaign-plan.md`)
- Confirm that the post/Reel for the week is ready or brief Kris on what's needed
- Schedule any Facebook Events for upcoming MWWT walks

**Weekly intention (Kris + Evan — 15 min call or async message):**
- One question answered: What is the single most important thing each person will accomplish this week?
- Any blockers surfaced early
- Keep it to 3 priorities each, maximum

---

### 3.2 Midweek (Tuesday–Thursday) — Execute

**Sales outreach (Kris — rolling):**
- Account visits prioritized to top 10 accounts (at least 2 in-person visits per week)
- New foodservice outreach: 1–2 targeted conversations per week during build phase
- Any delivery runs handled with a story card and a relationship touchpoint, not just a drop-off

**Tastings and sampling (as scheduled):**
- Tastings run on Wednesday or Thursday when possible (higher foot traffic at most specialty retailers)
- Every tasting logged: location, date, samples given, reaction, leads captured

**Content posting (Evan):**
- Instagram post goes live Tuesday or Wednesday (highest engagement windows)
- Reel posted separately if it's a recipe or walk clip
- Any Facebook Event for the following week's MWWT walk goes live Wednesday

**MWWT walk facilitation (Evan):**
- Walks typically held Saturday or Sunday morning — Wednesday is the reminder post and RSVP check
- DM outreach to regulars who haven't confirmed

---

### 3.3 Friday — Review and Reset

**KPI pulse check (Kris + Evan — 20 min, async or quick call):**
- Quick scan of the week's numbers: orders received, DTC traffic, Instagram reach on any posts that went live, account interactions
- Nothing formal — this is a gut-check, not a report
- Note anything that surprised you (good or bad)

**Learnings capture (each person, 10 min):**
- One thing that worked this week
- One thing that didn't
- One thing to try differently next week
- Written in the weekly log (Notion or shared doc) — takes 10 minutes, compounds over the year

**Next week setup (Evan — 15 min):**
- Confirm next week's content slot is planned and assets exist
- Check if any account visits need to be booked
- Review upcoming walk or event dates and confirm logistics

## 4. Monthly Rhythm

The monthly cadence is where the week-to-week activity gets interpreted. This is where patterns become visible — which accounts are growing, which channels are working, what the cash position actually says.

**Monthly business review (Kris + Evan — 60–90 min, first week of each month):**

*Inputs (pulled before the meeting):*
- KPI table from `02-company-goals-okrs.md` — updated for the prior month
- Revenue by channel (retail, DTC, foodservice)
- Account reorder log — any accounts that haven't reordered in 60+ days flagged
- Instagram Insights export — reach, engagement, profile visits, bio link clicks
- Klaviyo report — list size, welcome sequence conversion, monthly email open/click rates

*Agenda:*
1. **Numbers first (20 min):** Walk the KPI table. What's on track? What's off? No editorializing yet — just the numbers.
2. **What's working (15 min):** Which accounts, channels, or content formats are over-performing? What caused it? How do we do more of it?
3. **What's not working (15 min):** What underperformed? What's the honest diagnosis — wrong channel, wrong message, wrong timing, or not enough volume?
4. **Adjustments for next month (15 min):** One or two specific changes based on the above. Not a complete strategy overhaul — a calibration.
5. **Cash and inventory check (10 min):** Revenue vs. expenses. Inventory on hand vs. anticipated orders. Any near-term cash constraints?

*Outputs:*
- Updated KPI table
- 1–2 adjustments noted in the operating log
- Any flags for quarterly review

**Light S&OP (Kris — 30 min, standalone):**
- Forecast next month's production volume based on reorder velocity and any upcoming events or promos
- Confirm ingredient stock covers the forecast
- Flag any supplier lead time issues

**Content retrospective (Evan — 30 min, standalone):**
- Which posts in the prior month performed best and why?
- Is the content calendar for next month planned and realistic for the team's bandwidth?
- Any assets that need to be filmed or created in the next 2–3 weeks?

**Monthly email send (Evan — 2 hours total including writing):**
- One email to the Klaviyo list: what's been happening, what's coming, one story from the trail or table, soft purchase CTA
- Subject line tested with 2 options if list is large enough for A/B
- Sent in the last week of the month

## 5. Quarterly Rhythm

The quarterly review is the most important recurring ritual in the business. It is where the strategy meets reality and where honest course corrections happen before small problems become large ones.

**Quarterly strategy review (Kris + Evan — 3–4 hours, end of each quarter):**

*Inputs:*
- `01-strategy-2026-roadmap.md` — read it again before the meeting
- `02-company-goals-okrs.md` — score every KR against its target
- Prior quarter's monthly reviews — what patterns showed up consistently?
- Competitive scan: any new products, local brands, or market moves worth noting?

*Agenda:*
1. **OKR scorecard (45 min):** Score every KR. Red (off track), Yellow (at risk), Green (on track). No spin — be honest.
2. **What the data says (30 min):** What did the KPI trends over 3 months reveal that single-month snapshots didn't?
3. **What we'd do differently (30 min):** With the benefit of the past quarter's experience, what would we change about the strategy? What assumptions were wrong?
4. **Big bets for next quarter (45 min):** What are the 2–3 highest-leverage moves in the next 90 days? What are we explicitly NOT doing?
5. **Roadmap update (30 min):** Does `01-strategy-2026-roadmap.md` need to be updated? Make the edits in the meeting, not later.
6. **OKR refresh for next quarter (30 min):** Update any KRs that need to change based on the above. Keep the objectives stable if the direction is still right; adjust the KRs to reflect new targets or timelines.

*Outputs:*
- Updated `01-strategy-2026-roadmap.md`
- Updated `02-company-goals-okrs.md` with refreshed KRs
- Quarterly review summary written up in 1 page and saved in the operating log

**Quarterly financial review (Kris — standalone, can be same session):**
- 3-month revenue by channel vs. forecast
- Gross margin by channel — is it improving?
- Cash flow — what does the next 90 days look like?
- Any capital needs? If BDC conversation has been initiated, status update.

## 6. Meetings & Artifacts

### Weekly Intention Sync

| | |
|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Align on the week's priorities; surface blockers early |
| **Owner** | Alternating — Kris one week, Evan the next |
| **Participants** | Kris + Evan |
| **Format** | 15-minute call OR async Slack/text message |
| **Inputs** | Each person's 3 priorities for the week |
| **Outputs** | Shared understanding of who's doing what; any handoffs flagged |
| **Decisions made** | Priority conflicts resolved; any urgent account or production issues escalated |

---

### Monthly Business Review

| | |
|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Review KPIs, channels, and content performance; calibrate for next month |
| **Owner** | Evan (facilitates); Kris (owns numbers) |
| **Participants** | Kris + Evan |
| **Format** | 60–90 minute video call or in-person |
| **Inputs** | KPI table, revenue by channel, Instagram Insights, Klaviyo report, account reorder log |
| **Outputs** | Updated KPI table, 1–2 documented adjustments, cash/inventory status |
| **Decisions made** | Channel adjustments, sampling priorities, content focus for next month |

---

### Quarterly Strategy Review

| | |
|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Score OKRs, identify course corrections, refresh roadmap and OKRs for next quarter |
| **Owner** | Evan (facilitates); Kris (owns strategy decisions) |
| **Participants** | Kris + Evan |
| **Format** | 3–4 hour session — in person preferred |
| **Inputs** | Roadmap doc, OKR doc, prior 3 monthly review summaries, competitive scan |
| **Outputs** | Updated roadmap, updated OKRs, quarterly summary (1 page), big bets for next 90 days |
| **Decisions made** | Strategy pivots, channel opens/closes, capital allocation, partnership priorities |

---

### Ad Hoc Decision Review

| | |
|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Review any decision that touches the four guardrails (production quality, key person risk, brand integrity, channel infrastructure) |
| **Owner** | Whoever surfaces the issue |
| **Participants** | Kris + Evan (minimum) |
| **Format** | Async message is fine for low stakes; call required for anything that could affect the business model |
| **Inputs** | Description of the decision, the risk it triggers, and a proposed path forward |
| **Outputs** | Go/no-go decision with rationale documented |
| **Decisions made** | Guardrail exceptions (rare and deliberate) or confirmation that the guardrail holds |
