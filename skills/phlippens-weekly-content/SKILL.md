# Phlippens Weekly Content Skill

This skill drafts weekly Instagram content for the Phlippens brand and the Men Who Walk & Talk (MWWT) community account. It takes the week's pillar focus and a real story from Kris, and produces ready-to-review captions, optional Reel structure, and email subject lines — cutting drafting time from 45 minutes to under 10.

**Important:** Claude drafts; Kris approves. Every output here is a starting point for Kris's review — not final copy. If it doesn't sound like something he'd actually say, it goes back. That's the deal.

---

## What You Need to Run This

Before drafting, collect:

1. **This week's content pillar** — one of: Product / Purpose / Recipe / Behind the Scenes / Community
2. **The hook or story thread** — one line describing what the post is about or the angle you're working
3. **Real story detail from Kris** — voice note transcript, bullet points, or a few sentences in his words. The more real and specific, the better the output. "The tasting at Vincenzo's — 40 people tried it, one guy said it tasted like his dad's BBQ" beats "we ran a tasting."
4. **Post type** — Instagram static post, Reel, or carousel. If a Reel, note that so the skill generates a visual structure.
5. **Email week?** — Is this week an email broadcast? If yes, the skill will also output subject line options.

If the user hasn't provided all of these, ask for what's missing before drafting. A specific real story produces a dramatically better output than a generic request.

## Brand Voice Reference

Keep this front of mind for every piece of copy. Voice drift toward generic marketing is the most common failure mode.

**Phlippens:**
- Warm, direct, honest, slightly cheeky
- Talks like a person, not a brand
- Never corporate, never generic "foodie brand" voice
- The product earns its place by being real — so the copy has to be too
- Examples of right tone: "Smoked it ourselves. It shows." / "You'll put this on things you never thought to put sauce on." / "Kris started making this because he couldn't find something honest."
- Examples of wrong tone: "Elevate your culinary experience." / "Artisanal flavors crafted with care." / "A taste journey awaits."

**MWWT (Men Who Walk & Talk):**
- No pressure. Just blokes and conversation.
- Honest, low-key, bloke-ish
- Anti-performance-culture — this is a place to show up as you are
- Never aspirational-fitness-brand energy, never life-coach energy
- Examples of right tone: "No agenda. No cameras. Just walking." / "Show up. Walk. Talk if you want." / "First walk was five guys. Doesn't need to be more than that."

## The 5 Content Pillars

| Pillar | What it covers | Formats that work well |
|--------|---------------|----------------------|
| **Product** | The sauce itself — hand-smoked process, Ontario ingredients, versatility | Process Reel, "5 ways to use it" carousel, close-up with context |
| **Purpose** | Phlippen the Script — mental health mission, anti-performance-culture, "take the lid off" | Kris to camera, quote cards, honest text posts |
| **Recipe** | Practical, low-barrier recipes showing the product earning its place | Recipe Reel (phone, real kitchen, no styling), step-by-step carousel |
| **Behind the Scenes** | Real process — smoking, bottling, deliveries, market runs, account visits | Stories-first, raw clips, honest voiceovers |
| **Community** | MWWT walks, golf tournament, retailer stories, customer reactions | Walk recap, retailer spotlight, tasting moments, KIPR clips |

Monthly target: 2 Product + 1 Purpose + 3 Recipe + 2 BTS + 2 Community = ~10 posts/month across both accounts, with at least 4 Reels.

## Output Format

Produce the following, in this order:

### 1. Phlippens Instagram Captions (always)
Write **3 caption options** for Phlippens Instagram. Each should:
- Be max 150 words
- Start with a strong hook in the first line — this is the most important sentence, it's what stops the scroll
- Sound like Kris, not like a brand
- End with a low-friction CTA or a line that invites engagement (question, statement, nothing — never "tap the link in bio" unless there's a real reason to)

Label them Option A, B, C. Note briefly what angle each takes so Kris can pick fast.

### 2. MWWT Instagram Caption (if community-relevant)
Write **1 caption** for the MWWT account if the content connects to community, walks, or the mental health mission. MWWT voice only — honest, no-pressure, bloke-ish. If the week's content doesn't call for a MWWT post, say so briefly rather than forcing one.

### 3. Reel Structure (if post type is Reel)
Write a **3–5 beat visual structure**:
- Beat 1: Hook (what to film, what's said in the first 2 seconds)
- Beats 2–4: What happens (actions, cuts, words)
- Beat 5: Close / CTA

Keep it phone-filmable. No props, no styling, no equipment Kris doesn't own.

### 4. Email Subject Lines (if it's an email week)
Write **2 subject line options** — one curiosity-driven, one direct. Max 7 words each. Also write one preview line (the text that shows under the subject in the inbox, ~10 words).

## Quality Checks Before Outputting

Before finalizing the draft, run through these mentally:

- **Voice check:** Does any line sound like it came from a marketing agency? Rewrite it.
- **Specificity check:** Is there at least one specific detail from the real story? Generic captions don't convert; specific ones do.
- **Hook check:** Would you stop scrolling on that first line? If not, rewrite the hook.
- **Length check:** Are captions under 150 words? Email subject under 7 words?
- **Pillar alignment:** Does the content clearly serve the chosen pillar?

## What to Watch

**Voice drift** is the main risk. If outputs start sounding polished, elevated, or generic, add "raw and honest, not slick" to the prompt. The brand lives in the real story — if the real story detail is thin, ask Kris for more before drafting.

**MWWT over-posting** — MWWT content only works when it's genuinely relevant to the community angle. Don't force a MWWT caption just to fill the slot.

**Reel over-engineering** — The best Phlippens Reels are simple. Three things happening, one honest voice, no B-roll. If the structure has more than 5 beats, simplify.
