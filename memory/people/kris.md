# Kris

**Role:** Founder, Phlippens
**Also known as:** Kris
**Part-owner**

## What Kris Does
- Runs all production (hand-smoked process, jarring, labelling)
- Handles all retail sales and account relationships
- Does all deliveries
- The face and voice of the brand

## The Brand Voice Lives in Kris
All content drafted by Evan or Claude must be reviewed by Kris before going live. Not for grammar — for truth. If it doesn't sound like something he'd actually say, it goes back.

## Communication Style
- Direct, honest, real
- Not corporate, not polished, not performative
- The brand tone IS Kris's tone

## Context
- The hand-smoked production process is founder-led and quality-obsessed — this is the moat
- Kris built the retail base of 50+ accounts through personal relationships
- The restaurant anchor story happened organically — a chef reached out
- Part of the MWWT movement — walks as a participant, not just a promoter

## Constraints
- Kris is running production, sales, AND delivery — bandwidth is the primary constraint
- Every strategy decision must be achievable with Kris + one part-time consultant (Evan)
- Production is a natural ceiling on volume — quality cannot be compromised to scale

## Key Beliefs (brand guardrails)
- Hand-smoked process is non-negotiable — no shortcuts on ingredient quality
- The brand story must stay anchored to real people and real practices
- "Suffering quietly needs to stop" — this is personal, not a marketing angle
