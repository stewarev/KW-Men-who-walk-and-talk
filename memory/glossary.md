## Retail & Sales Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| velocity | Units per store per week. The primary retail health metric. |
| A account | High velocity, featured placement, engaged owner. Monthly in-person visits. |
| B account | Moderate velocity, standard shelf. Quarterly tastings, monthly check-in. |
| C account | Low reorder frequency, poor placement. 60-day check-in and fit assessment. |
| featured placement | Counter, end cap, or signage — not just stocked on a shelf |
| tasting | In-store demo. Product + story card. Measure before/after velocity. |
| reorder frequency | Average days between orders at a given account |
| 50+ accounts | Current active reordering retail base in Waterloo Region |
