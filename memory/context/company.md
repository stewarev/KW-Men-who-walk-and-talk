# Phlippens — Company Context

## What Phlippens Is
An Ontario-made, hand-smoked condiment brand built around the belief that food sparks real connection — and that men suffering quietly while pretending everything is fine needs to stop. The product is the permission slip. The community is the proof.

## The Ecosystem (Four Layers)
1. **Phlippens** (Product) — hand-smoked garlic & onion condiment. Gets people to the table.
2. **Keep It Phlippen Real** (Content) — story engine. Real people, real conversations.
3. **Phlippen the Script** (Purpose) — the WHY. Anti-performance-culture belief system.
4. **Men Who Walk & Talk** (Community) — offline movement. No cameras, no agenda, no performance.

The imitation barrier: no condiment brand has the community. No men's community has the product.

## Brand Tone
- Warm, direct, honest, slightly cheeky
- Never corporate, never generic "foodie brand"
- Talks like a person, not a brand
