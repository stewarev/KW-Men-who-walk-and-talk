# Phlippens Company OS

**Status:** Active — built May 2026
**Owner:** Evan (architecture) + Kris (content owner)

## What It Is
A full operating system for Phlippens across 7 business domains. 10 system documents plus 3 brand files. Built to: (1) give Kris and Evan a shared source of truth, (2) make AI assistance consistent and contextual, (3) create institutional memory that doesn't live only in people's heads.

## The 7 Domains
1. Strategy & Leadership
2. Brand, Marketing & Community
3. Sales & Trade (Retail, Foodservice, DTC)
4. Operations & Supply Chain
5. Finance & Admin
6. People & Partners
7. Data, Systems & AI

## Key Decisions Made in Building This OS
- **ICP narrowed to KW specialty retail + food service** for 2026. Not mass consumer.
- **Restaurant anchor story is the lead message** everywhere — not the founder story. The founder story supports.
- **3 channels max** — Instagram, Facebook Events, Klaviyo. Bandwidth reality for 2-person team.
- **Layer 1 must happen before Layer 4** — foundation (unit economics, restaurant story film, Klaviyo setup, account audit) before public campaign execution.
- **Existing 50+ accounts = activation focus, not new account acquisition.**
- **Mabel's Bakery** (Toronto, Phlippen Breakfast Sandwich) is the second foodservice proof point — opens a Toronto conversation eventually.
