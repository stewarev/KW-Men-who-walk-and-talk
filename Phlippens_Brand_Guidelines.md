# Phlippens Brand Guidelines - January 2026

## Who is Phlippens?
Phlippens is more than just a condiment; it's an invitation to gather, share meals, and connect [cite: 692]. Founded by Kris, every bottle carries a smoky flavor and a piece of his story, inviting consumers to join a community built on authenticity and real talk [cite: 694, 695, 697, 698].

### Product Highlights
* **Locally Sourced:** Ingredients are locally grown and all-natural [cite: 705, 712, 715].
* **Made in Ontario:** Proudly Canadian and made in Ontario [cite: 684, 709, 712].
* **Signature Flavor:** Garlic and onions are gently hand-smoked with fruitwood [cite: 706, 714].
* **Dietary Friendly:** The products are Phlippen gluten-free [cite: 706, 713].
* **Versatile:** Can be used as a dip, spread, marinade, or ingredient [cite: 707].

## Our Phlippen Mission & Vision
* **Mission:** To take the lid off normal, spark conversations, and build a community where everyone feels like family [cite: 725].
* **Vision:** To build a bold, unapologetic brand that becomes a household name, creating a movement where food sparks community [cite: 734, 736].

## Brand Identity

### Logos
* **Primary Logo (The Wordmark):** The main identifier for the brand. It should lead in most applications and must never be altered [cite: 749, 750, 751].
* **Primary Logo (The Face):** A secondary graphic mark used as a supporting asset in social media or repeat touchpoints where brand awareness already exists [cite: 756, 758].
* **Secondary Logo (The Lock-up):** A combination of the wordmark and the face graphic [cite: 763].
