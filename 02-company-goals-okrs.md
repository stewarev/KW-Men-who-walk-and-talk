# Phlippens Company Goals & OKRs

*Last updated: May 2026 | Owner: Kris (Founder) | Review cadence: Monthly KPI review, Quarterly OKR refresh*

---

## 1. How to Use This Doc

This is the single source of truth for annual and quarterly goals at Phlippens. Every decision about where to spend time, money, and attention should trace back to something in here. If it doesn't, either the work shouldn't be done or the OKRs need to be updated.

**How it connects to other docs:**
- Objectives come from `01-strategy-2026-roadmap.md` — if a goal isn't in the roadmap, it probably shouldn't be an OKR yet
- KPIs in Section 4 feed the weekly/monthly review in `03-operating-cadence.md`
- Domain OKRs in Section 3 are the working targets for each part of the business

**Rules of the road:**
- Objectives are qualitative direction-setters. Key Results are measurable and time-bound.
- A Key Result without a number is a task, not a KR. Push until it's quantified.
- No more than 4 company objectives at a time. No more than 3–4 KRs per objective.
- OKRs are reviewed monthly (are we on track?) and refreshed quarterly (do they still reflect our priorities?).

## 2. Annual Company Objectives (2026)

### Objective 1: Become the dominant local condiment brand in Waterloo Region

*Why:* Before expanding geography or channels, the KW market must be proven. Depth before breadth.

- **KR1:** Grow active reordering retail accounts from 50+ to 65 by Q4 2026
- **KR2:** Achieve featured placement (counter, end cap, or signage) in 20+ accounts by end of Q3
- **KR3:** 5+ foodservice operators in KW featuring Phlippens by name on their menu by end of 2026
- **KR4:** At least 1 local editorial media story (not a press release pickup) by end of Q3 2026

---

### Objective 2: Build an owned customer relationship engine through DTC and email

*Why:* Retail accounts are rented distribution. DTC and email are owned. The goal is to shift enough customer relationships to owned channels to survive any retail disruption.

- **KR1:** Klaviyo welcome sequence live and converting at 10%+ to first purchase by end of Q2 2026
- **KR2:** Email list at 500+ subscribers by end of Q4 2026 (150+ by end of Q3)
- **KR3:** 30%+ repeat purchase rate within 90 days of first DTC order
- **KR4:** At least 1 recipe Reel per month generating measurable traffic to phlippens.com

---

### Objective 3: Prove the community-product flywheel with one centrepiece activation

*Why:* The four-layer ecosystem (Product → Content → Purpose → Community) is the brand's long-term imitation barrier. The golf tournament is the first test of whether it compounds in the real world.

- **KR1:** KW Suicide Prevention Golf Tournament — 150+ people sampled
- **KR2:** 15+ new MWWT walk participants signed up at or via the tournament
- **KR3:** Tournament content reaches 5,000+ Reel views across both brand accounts
- **KR4:** KWSP cross-tags and amplifies both brands to their audience

---

### Objective 4: Document the business model to support future capital and scale decisions

*Why:* "Prove the model, then pour gas" requires knowing what the model actually costs to run and what it returns. Without documented unit economics, capital conversations (BDC, investors, distributors) are impossible to have credibly.

- **KR1:** COGS per unit documented by end of May 2026
- **KR2:** Channel-level margins (retail, DTC, foodservice) calculated and compared by end of Q2 2026
- **KR3:** Break-even tasting event calculation completed and used in sampling decisions
- **KR4:** One-page financial summary ready for external conversation (BDC, buyer, investor) by end of Q3 2026

## 3. Domain-Level OKRs

### 3.1 Brand, Marketing & Community

**Objective:** Make Phlippens the most talked-about local food brand in Waterloo Region through authentic content and community presence — not paid advertising.

Key Results:
- Combined Instagram following grows by 1,000+ net new (quality, KW-local, engaged)
- Engagement rate holds at 5%+ on Reels and 3%+ on static posts
- Keep It Phlippen Real launches as a named series with at least 3 published episodes by end of Q3
- Men Who Walk & Talk reaches 30+ total walk participants across all KW walks by end of Q3
- At least 1 piece of earned media coverage (radio, local press, community blog) by end of Q3

---

### 3.2 Sales & Trade

**Objective:** Deepen Phlippens' position in existing KW accounts and selectively open high-fit new accounts — velocity and placement before volume.

Key Results:
- Top 10 accounts visited in person at least once per month with fresh product and a story
- 20+ accounts upgraded to featured placement (counter, end cap, or signage) by end of Q3
- 3+ new foodservice accounts opened with Phlippens featured by name on the menu
- Reorder frequency tracked for all 50+ accounts; average reorder cycle documented
- 65 total active reordering accounts by end of Q4

---

### 3.3 Operations & Supply Chain

**Objective:** Keep the hand-smoked production process scalable, consistent, and documented so it doesn't become a growth bottleneck.

Key Results:
- Full production process documented (written SOP) by end of Q2 2026
- Lead time from order to delivery held at 2 weeks or under as volume grows
- Ingredient supplier relationships documented with at least one backup for each key input
- Explore co-production or production support options by end of Q3 — decision made by Q4
- Zero stockouts at top 10 accounts in Q3 and Q4

---

### 3.4 Finance & Admin

**Objective:** Build financial visibility that supports confident decisions on pricing, sampling, and capital.

Key Results:
- COGS per unit documented and reviewed monthly by May 2026
- Monthly revenue tracked by channel (retail, DTC, foodservice) from June 2026 forward
- Break-even analysis for tasting events completed by end of Q2
- Cash flow reviewed monthly — no decisions made that would leave less than 60 days of operating cash
- BDC financing conversation initiated if production capacity becomes a binding constraint before end of Q4

---

### 3.5 People & Partners

**Objective:** Build a small, high-trust partner network that extends the team's capacity without adding fixed costs.

Key Results:
- KWSP partnership formalized (written agreement or MOU) before golf tournament
- At least 2 local media contacts cultivated as genuine relationships (not just press list names) by end of Q3
- One local photographer or videographer identified as a go-to collaborator for content by end of Q2
- Explore one part-time production helper for H2 2026 if revenue supports it — decision by end of Q3

---

### 3.6 Data, Systems & AI

**Objective:** Put the minimum viable data infrastructure in place to track what's working and make better decisions — without overbuilding before the business needs it.

Key Results:
- Klaviyo set up with welcome sequence and monthly broadcast by end of May 2026
- Instagram Insights tracked weekly — Reels reach, saves, profile visits, bio link clicks
- Sales by channel tracked in a simple spreadsheet or Notion table from June 2026 forward
- Account reorder log maintained with last order date, frequency, and placement quality for all 50+ accounts
- One AI session per month (with Claude) to review what the data says and what to adjust — documented in operating cadence
